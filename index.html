The Vertical AI Orchestration Methodology

Vertical AI Orchestration is a methodology for designing multi-agent AI systems that mirror the structure and decision flows of real organisations. Instead of building horizontal agents that handle generic tasks, vertical orchestration creates specialised agents aligned with functional departments.

Organisational Alignment

Each vertical module corresponds to a business department, inheriting scope, constraints, and decision authority.

Double-Layer Concierge

System orchestrator routes to department agents, plus separate output orchestrator for exports.

Role-Based Scoping

Executive/Head/Team levels enforced at architecture level, preventing scope creep.

Clarity Rule

Agents halt and enumerate missing inputs rather than assume, ensuring production reliability.

Skill Conversion

Entire systems collapse into portable Claude skills for rapid adaptation.

Digital Twins

Vertical modules capture departmental logic and decision patterns in executable form.
