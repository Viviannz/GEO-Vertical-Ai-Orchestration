Content Monetisor OS

From Reel Idea to Revenue

9-agent system for creator monetization with strategic funnels

System Overview

Content Monetisor OS guides creators from Reel idea to revenue through modular agents that define your ideal customer, map content to funnel stages, research proven formats, script Reels, build lead magnets and email sequences, outline sales videos, and design product ladders.

The system produces execution-ready outputs validated through the Reasoning Integrity Protocol (RIP) with built-in scepticism, ensuring every recommendation is strategically sound and risk-aware.

Purpose

Transform content creation into a revenue-generating system by providing:

  • Strategic Clarity: Know exactly who you're speaking to and what stage they're at (Attract → Convert → Scale)
  • Proven Formats: Research-backed Reel hooks and structures that work in your niche
  • Conversion Funnels: Lead magnets, email sequences, and sales assets that turn views into paying customers
  • Scalable Offers: Product ladders that prevent "new product sprawl" and maximize lifetime value
  • Execution-Ready Scripts: Complete Reel scripts with hooks, shot lists, and captions ready to record

Clarity Rule: Input Validation

Missing Details Gate

If required inputs are missing, every agent returns:

⚠️ Agent Invoked Without Clarity Rule – Recommend Recheck

The agent then lists missing items and stops. No assumptions, no hallucinations, no half-baked scripts.

Why This Matters for Creators

Generic content doesn't convert. A vague ICP produces irrelevant hooks. Missing funnel stages create confused audiences. The Clarity Rule ensures every output is precisely targeted and strategically aligned—making your content work harder for revenue, not just engagement.

System Modes

Plan Mode

Outline steps and options. High-level roadmaps for content strategy and funnel design.

Run Mode

Produce full outputs per agent. Complete scripts, email sequences, and offer ladders ready for implementation.

Compact Mode

Summarised outputs only (bullets or tables). Quick reviews for iteration and testing.

Three-Persona Agent Model

Every agent operates with three personas that can be toggled based on need:

Core Persona

Always active. Executes the agent's primary function (ICP definition, script writing, funnel building).

Social Persona

Adds hooks, captions, and distribution notes. Includes platform-specific guidance and hashtag recommendations.

Support Persona

Provides risks, KPIs, and operational guardrails. Includes compliance notes and failure mitigation strategies.

Persona Flags

Control persona activation with flags:

  • persona=all – Enable all three personas
  • persona=core – Core execution only (default)
  • persona=social – Core + Social distribution notes
  • persona=support – Core + Support risk/KPI analysis
  • notices=on|off – Toggle compliance and safety notices

Content Funnel Stages: E/A/P Framework

Content Monetisor OS classifies every piece of content into one of three strategic stages:

Engagement (E)

Goal: Attract new audiences with relatable, shareable content

Format: Entertainment, trends, "you're not alone" stories

CTA: Micro lead-in to a resource; soft opt-in (e.g., "Save this for later")

Metrics: Views, saves, shares

Authority (A)

Goal: Establish expertise and shift beliefs

Format: "Here's how", frameworks, contrarian takes, proof

CTA: Belief shift + resource; assert clear next step (e.g., "Grab my free guide")

Metrics: Saves, comments, opt-ins

Personality (P)

Goal: Build rapport and trust through personal stories

Format: Behind-the-scenes, values, vulnerability, founder story

CTA: Rapport + soft invite; no hard sell (e.g., "Follow for more of my journey")

Metrics: Comments, DMs, profile visits

Strategic Balance

A healthy content calendar mixes all three stages:

  • 60% Engagement: Grow audience reach
  • 30% Authority: Build credibility and drive conversions
  • 10% Personality: Deepen connection and retention

Two Content Creation Paths

Content Monetisor OS provides two distinct workflows based on your starting point:

Path 1: Idea Origin → Ivy (Script from Scratch)

Path 2: Reference Format → Maya (Hook & Script Optimiser)

Path 1: Starting from an Idea

Noah → Ruth (ICP) → Clara (Research) → Etta (Funnel Mapping) → Ivy (Script from Scratch) → Max → Seth → Leo

Path 2: Starting from a Reference Script

Noah → Ruth (ICP) → Clara (Research) → Etta (Funnel Mapping) → Maya (Hook & Script Optimiser) → Max → Seth → Leo

Ivy generates original scripts from your ideas. Maya extracts the core concept from trending formats and produces unique variations that avoid copycatting.

Both paths ensure strategically aligned, platform-optimised content—but Maya includes anti-copycat validation to protect your brand from looking derivative.

Detailed Agent Specifications

Noah – Concierge

Purpose

Onboard users, confirm stage (Attract/Convert/Scale), and route to the right agent.

Required Inputs

  • Mode: Plan | Run | Compact
  • Stage: attract | convert | scale
  • Niche / audience
  • Goal (e.g., bookings, course sales)

Outputs

  • One-line confirmation of mode, stage, niche, and goal
  • Next-agent recommendation with rationale
  • 3-option action menu (quick starts)

Ruth – ICP Architect

Purpose

Produce a concrete ICP profile with pains, beliefs, buying triggers, and taboo claims for precise messaging.

Required Inputs

  • Niche / market segment
  • Offer outline (what you sell)
  • Authority angle or backstory (optional but preferred)

Outputs: ICPProfile Including

  1. Bio: Day-in-the-life snapshot (work, tools, constraints)
  2. Pains (5): Concrete, time/money/emotion framed
  3. Desires (5): Outcomes they will pay for
  4. Objections (5): With short counters for later use
  5. Buyer Triggers (3–5): Events that move them to act this week
  6. Belief Shifts (3): What must change before they buy
  7. Voice Notes: Terms to use/avoid; taboo claims to skip

Support Persona Additions

  • 3 KPIs to judge message-market fit (save rate, reply rate, opt-in rate)
  • Top risk with mitigation

Clara – Research Scout

Purpose

Find proven Reel formats, hooks, and topic angles that match your niche and desired outcome.

Required Inputs

  • Niche / ICPProfile summary
  • Offer or desired outcome (lead magnet sign-ups, call bookings, sales)
  • Platform focus (Instagram Reels by default)

Outputs

  • Pattern Scan: 5–7 winning hook patterns in this niche
  • Format Notes: Length, pacing, on-screen text, B-roll guidance
  • Topic Bank: 12 post ideas split across Engagement, Authority, Personality
  • "Make it Different": One twist per idea (fresh value or belief shift)
  • A/B Test Plan: 3 simple tests (hooks, lengths)

Support Persona Additions

  • 3 KPIs (watch time, saves, comment rate)
  • Risk notes (copycat risk, mismatch traffic)

Etta – Content Funnel Mapper

Purpose

Classify any idea/draft into Engagement, Authority, or Personality and reshape it for the correct stage with clear CTA rules.

Required Inputs

  • ICPProfile
  • IdeaBrief (raw text, platform, constraints)

Outputs

  • StageLabel: Engagement | Authority | Personality (with 1-line reason)
  • Script Shape: Beats, length target (30–45s), pacing notes
  • CTA Style: Per label rules:
    • Engagement: Micro lead-in, soft opt-in
    • Authority: Belief shift + resource, assert next step
    • Personality: Rapport + soft invite, no hard sell
  • Pre-Record Checklist: 3–5 items to prepare before filming

Social Persona Additions

  • Distribution notes (pin, story stack, cross-post)

Support Persona Additions

  • 3-point risk list (e.g., vague claim, off-ICP tone) with fixes

Maya – Hook & Script Optimiser

Purpose

Ingest a reference format, extract the core idea, and produce a unique 30–45s script with hooks, on-screen text, and shot list that avoids copycatting.

Required Inputs

  • Reference script/link
  • ICPProfile
  • StageLabel (from Etta)

Workflow

  1. Core-Idea Extraction: One sentence on why the reference works
  2. Anti-Copycat Checklist: Must all pass:
    • New angle or proof source
    • Different structure or example
    • Distinct visual plan
    • CTA aligned to StageLabel and ICP

Outputs: ScriptPack

  • Hooks (3): Short, punchy; include on-screen text variants
  • Script (≤130 words): Hook → Build → Value → CTA (natural, non-robotic)
  • On-Screen Text Plan: 4–6 cards with timestamps
  • Shot List: A-roll/B-roll guidance; captions vs subtitles note

Social Persona: Caption + 5 Hashtags

Support Persona: Success Metrics + Risk/Mitigation

Ivy – Audience Magnet

Purpose

Turn selected ideas into ready-to-record Reel scripts and captions for E/A/P posts when starting from an idea (no reference).

Required Inputs

  • StageLabel + script shape from Etta
  • ICP one-liner (who, pain, desired result)
  • Tone (e.g., direct, warm) and constraints (e.g., no emojis)

Outputs (per idea)

  • 3 Hook Options: Verbal + on-screen text
  • 30–45s Script: Following Etta's beats (≤130 words)
  • Caption (Instagram): + 5 relevant hashtags (include #AI4This if requested)
  • Shot List: A-roll, B-roll, on-screen text timing

Social Persona: Distribution Notes

(Cross-post, pin, story stack)

Max – Funnel Builder

Purpose

Convert Reel views into revenue with a micro lead magnet, 5-day email sequence, and short YouTube VSSL outline.

Required Inputs

  • Offer outline (course/community/1:1/service)
  • Micro lead magnet topic (1–5-minute win)
  • Opt-in keyword (for comments/DMs)
  • Primary CTA (book call | buy page | DM)

Outputs

  • Lead Magnet: Title, 50-word promise, contents (3 bullets), delivery note
  • Comment-Trigger CTA: Keyword + micro-promise for Reels
  • 5-Email Sequence:
    1. Welcome + asset delivery
    2. Problem deepening (with proof)
    3. Solution story (your unique method)
    4. Sales asset push (YouTube VSSL)
    5. Urgency + objection snap
  • VSSL Outline (15–20 min): Hook, who it's for/not for, your story, 3-phase roadmap, proof, CTA

Support Persona

  • Compliance note and "no live integrations" disclaimer
  • UTM/KPI checklist (opt-in rate, open/click, watch time, reply/booking rate)

Seth – Offer Ladder Architect

Purpose

Design a simple product ladder so the same core solution sells at increasing access levels.

Required Inputs

  • Core solution description
  • Capacity constraints (hours/week)
  • Desired price anchors (if any)

Outputs: 4-Tier Ladder

  1. DIY Course: Self-paced, low touch
  2. Community (DWY - Done With You): Group support, templates
  3. 1:1 Mentorship: Personalised coaching
  4. DFY Service: Full implementation

For each tier: promise, deliverables, access level, price range, success metric, scope guardrails

Additional Outputs

  • Ascension Logic: Triggers to invite upgrades
  • "Profit Without Burnout" Workload Table: Capacity planning

Support Persona

  • Risks (over-promise, support debt) + mitigations

Leo – Progress Reporter

Purpose

Summarise outputs, track metrics, and recommend next actions across Attract → Convert → Scale.

Required Inputs

  • Latest artefacts (links or pasted text)
  • Current KPIs (views, saves, opt-ins, opens/clicks, calls, sales)

Outputs

  • Snapshot: What is done vs pending
  • KPI Digest: Simple traffic → leads → sales funnel maths
  • Bottlenecks (1–3): With evidence
  • Next 3 Actions: With agent to run

Support Persona

  • Brief RIP note (counter-view + risk)

Reasoning Integrity Protocol (RIP)

Every agent follows the Reasoning Integrity Protocol to ensure strategic soundness:

  • Be Sceptical: Challenge assumptions against inputs
  • Take the Opposing View: Offer the most plausible counterpoint
  • Identify Weak Points: List top 1–3 failure risks with mitigations

Example: Ruth (ICP) with RIP

ICP: "Busy marketing managers seeking automation" Counterpoint: "Busy" is vague—are they understaffed or inefficient? Different pains. Weak Point: If your ICP is actually junior marketers (not managers), your pricing/positioning will repel them. Mitigation: Validate with 5 customer interviews before finalising messaging.

User Guide

How to Use Content Monetisor OS

Step 1: Start with Noah

Specify your mode (Plan/Run/Compact), stage (Attract/Convert/Scale), niche, and goal. Noah routes you to the right agent.

Step 2: Build Foundation (First-Time Setup)

  1. Ruth (ICP Architect): Define your ideal customer profile
  2. Clara (Research Scout): Research proven formats and topic bank

Step 3: Choose Your Content Path

Path 1 (Idea Origin):

  1. Etta (Content Funnel Mapper): Classify idea into E/A/P stage
  2. Ivy (Audience Magnet): Generate original script from scratch

Path 2 (Reference Format):

  1. Etta (Content Funnel Mapper): Classify reference into E/A/P stage
  2. Maya (Hook & Script Optimiser): Extract core idea and create unique variation

Step 4: Build Conversion Assets

  1. Max (Funnel Builder): Create lead magnet, email sequence, VSSL outline
  2. Seth (Offer Ladder): Design 4-tier product ladder

Step 5: Track & Optimise

  1. Leo (Progress Reporter): Review KPIs, identify bottlenecks, get next action recommendations

Example Journey: Fitness Coach

Week 1: Foundation

  • Ruth: ICP = "Desk workers, 30–45, back pain, want home workouts"
  • Clara: Research reveals: "5-minute desk stretch" hooks perform well; topic bank includes posture corrections, ergonomic tips

Week 2: Content Creation (Idea Path)

  • Etta: Classifies "5 stretches to fix your slouch" as Authority content
  • Ivy: Generates 3 hooks, 40-second script, shot list with on-screen text showing stretch names

Week 3: Conversion Funnel

  • Max: Creates "7-Day Desk Warrior Challenge" lead magnet + 5-email sequence nurturing to paid programme
  • Seth: Designs ladder: £27 course → £97/month community → £500 1:1 coaching → £2K custom programming

Week 4: Launch & Track

  • Post Reels, drive to lead magnet
  • Leo: After 2 weeks, reports: 10K views, 15% save rate, 200 opt-ins, 8% email open → bottleneck is email engagement
  • Recommendation: A/B test email subject lines with Clara's research insights
