System Overview

SaaS Reality Check System validates your AI startup idea before you write a single line of code. Using 5 specialised agents, it assesses your niche, problem, leverage, and moat to give you a clear build/delay/pivot verdict.

This system stops founders from wasting months building GPT wrappers that will never gain traction. It validates through risk-first analysis and ensures real problems, real leverage, and defensible positioning exist before you commit to building.

⚠️ Reasoning Integrity Protocol (RIP)

This system challenges assumptions of scalability and defensibility.

  • Validates through risk-first analysis
  • No SaaS output unless real problem and leverage exist
  • No GPT wrapper illusions or vibe coding traps
  • Objective validation over false encouragement

Purpose

Prevent founders from building AI tools that will fail in the market. The system:

  • Maps Problems: Ensures your idea solves a sharp, urgent pain point
  • Detects Moats: Evaluates cloneability risk and technical defensibility
  • Checks Market Reality: Analyses saturation, timing, and competitive noise
  • Assesses Leverage: Validates whether you have the audience and distribution to win
  • Provides Verdict: Clear build/validate/delay recommendation with risk summary

Detailed Agent Specifications

Clara – StoryMapper

Purpose

Turn founder idea into a structured pain → goal → build → outcome arc.

When to Activate

When a user submits a SaaS or product idea and wants to see if it maps to a clear problem.

Prompt Template

Running [Clara – StoryMapper]... Tell me your idea as simply as possible. Then tell me: - Who it's for - What pain they're feeling - What your tool helps them do better I'll return a clear breakdown of: 1. Core Pain 2. Manual Workarounds (if any) 3. Your Proposed Fix 4. What "Success" looks like 5. Whether the problem is sharp and urgent

Inputs

  • Founder's idea description
  • Target user/customer
  • Pain point description
  • Proposed solution

Outputs

  1. Core Pain: Clear statement of the problem
  2. Manual Workarounds: How people currently solve it
  3. Your Proposed Fix: How your tool improves on workarounds
  4. Success Definition: What "better" looks like
  5. Problem Assessment: Is the pain sharp and urgent enough?
Red Flag: If the problem is vague, theoretical, or lacks urgency, Clara will flag it immediately. Weak problem = weak foundation.

Ethan – MoatDetector

Purpose

Test for uniqueness, defensibility, tech stack risk, and cloneability.

When to Activate

Once a product idea has been mapped and is ready for risk analysis.

Prompt Template

Running [Ethan – MoatDetector]... Paste a short summary of your tool. Tell me: - What powers it (APIs, LLMs, scrapers, custom code?) - What makes it hard to replicate? - Is there any owned IP or data? I'll score your idea on: - Cloneability Risk - Infra Drag - Moat Strength - API Dependence With a colour-coded verdict (Green, Yellow, Red)

Workflow

  1. Analyse technical architecture (APIs, models, infrastructure)
  2. Identify cloneability factors (how easily can competitors replicate?)
  3. Assess owned assets (proprietary data, algorithms, networks)
  4. Score moat strength and infrastructure dependency

Outputs

  • Cloneability Risk: Low / Medium / High
  • Infra Drag: How much infrastructure cost eats margin?
  • Moat Strength: Defensibility score (proprietary data, network effects, unique algorithms)
  • API Dependence: Risk level if relying on third-party APIs

🚨 GPT Wrapper Warning

If your product is just API calls to OpenAI/Claude with no unique data, network, or algorithm, Ethan will flag it as high cloneability risk.

Wrapping LLM APIs is not a moat. You need defensible differentiation.

Maya – MarketRealityCheck

Purpose

Analyse market saturation, investor sentiment, and competitor noise.

When to Activate

Once the tool or idea has been roughly validated.

Prompt Template

Running [Maya – MarketRealityCheck]... Tell me the niche or vertical you're targeting. Then tell me what your product does, who it's for, and what outcome it promises. I'll scan for: - GPT wrapper graveyard comparisons - Investor attention or withdrawal - Competitive noise levels - Opportunity timing Expect: → A Market Saturation score → Timing window estimate → "Hype Trap" warning if applicable

Analysis Areas

  • Market Saturation: How crowded is this space?
  • Investor Sentiment: Is funding flowing in or drying up?
  • Competitive Noise: How many similar tools already exist?
  • Timing Analysis: Is this early, peak, or late?

Outputs

  • Market Saturation Score: Low / Medium / High / Overcrowded
  • Timing Window: Early opportunity / Growing space / Maturing / Saturated
  • Hype Trap Warning: If everyone is building the same thing
  • Investor Attention: Active funding or cooling interest

⚠️ Hype Trap Detection

Maya flags when your idea falls into a category where hundreds of founders are building identical tools (e.g., "AI email assistant," "AI meeting notes," "AI writing tool").

Being in a hype trap doesn't mean automatic failure—but you need exceptional distribution or niche positioning to win.

Isaac – LeverageStack

Purpose

Assess if founder has audience, distribution, niche insight, or timing advantage.

When to Activate

After the user has described themselves or their existing assets.

Prompt Template

Running [Isaac – LeverageStack]... Answer a few questions: - Do you have a following or audience? Where? - What niche do you know deeply? - Have you launched anything before? - How do people find your work today? I'll return a Leverage Profile with: - Audience Score - Timing Fit - Platform Edge - Content Leverage + One-line recommendation on next best move

Leverage Factors

  1. Audience/Following: Do you have distribution? (Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletter, community)
  2. Niche Expertise: Do you deeply understand your target user?
  3. Platform Edge: Are you building on/for a platform you already dominate?
  4. Content Leverage: Can you attract users through content marketing?
  5. Launch History: Have you successfully shipped before?

Outputs

Leverage Profile:

  • Audience Score: None / Small / Growing / Established
  • Timing Fit: Are you positioned to catch the wave?
  • Platform Edge: Do you have insider knowledge or network?
  • Content Leverage: Can you reach users organically?

Next Best Move: One-line tactical recommendation based on leverage gaps.

✅ High Leverage Founders

Founders with existing audience, deep niche knowledge, and distribution channels can succeed even in crowded markets.

If you have leverage, your odds improve dramatically—even with a "red flag" idea.

Leo – BuildOrNot

Purpose

Generate a no-BS final verdict: validate, pivot, or delay SaaS build.

When to Activate

After all prior agents have run and returned scores or feedback.

Prompt Template

Running [Leo – BuildOrNot]... Paste in your results from: - Clara (Problem arc) - Ethan (Moat score) - Maya (Market signal) - Isaac (Leverage) I'll give you a simple build verdict: 🟢 Proceed 🟡 Validate further 🔴 Delay or pivot Plus: → A one-paragraph investor-style summary → Key risks → Next step checklist

Verdict Logic

Verdict Criteria Meaning
🟢 Proceed Strong problem + moat + leverage Build immediately with confidence
🟡 Validate Solid problem but weak moat OR leverage gaps Validate demand before full build
🔴 Delay Weak problem OR high clone risk + no leverage Pivot or build leverage first

Outputs

  1. Build Verdict: 🟢 Proceed / 🟡 Validate / 🔴 Delay
  2. Investor-Style Summary: One-paragraph assessment as if pitching to investor
  3. Key Risks: Top 3 threats to success
  4. Next Step Checklist: Specific actions based on verdict

Example Verdict Output

Verdict: 🟡 Validate further

Summary: Your product solves a real pain for freelance designers (sharp problem), but the market is crowded with Figma plugins and design assistants. You have decent Twitter following (3K) and niche knowledge, but moat is weak—no proprietary data or unique tech. Recommend validating demand with a landing page + waitlist before full build.

Key Risks:

  1. High cloneability—Figma could build this natively
  2. Market saturation—dozens of design AI tools launched in 2024
  3. API dependency—relying entirely on OpenAI/Anthropic

Next Steps:

  1. Build landing page with clear value prop
  2. Drive 500+ waitlist signups via Twitter content
  3. Interview 20 target users to validate pain intensity
  4. If validation strong, build MVP; if weak, pivot to adjacent niche

FAQ

Q: Can I use this for non-AI tools?

Yes. But it's especially good for AI tool validation.

Q: What if I want to build anyway?

Use Leo to get a risk-aware roadmap. Some build paths are still viable even with yellow/red flags—if you have strong leverage.

Q: What if I already built it?

Run the system to see if it's salvageable. Leo will provide pivot recommendations if needed.

Q: What's a "GPT wrapper graveyard"?

The hundreds of abandoned AI tools that were just thin layers over ChatGPT/Claude APIs with no moat, distribution, or unique value. Most died within 6 months of launch.
